What is faithful. The glyph outlines, chrome gradient, neon glow, word-wrap and auto-sizing come from synthfont.h; the skies, striped sun, ridges, skylines, grid, palms and scanlines from scene.h. Every pixel is quantised to RGB565 exactly as the panel does it, and the boot screen prints through the same Adafruit GFX 5×7 font the board uses. Fortunes are sampled from the real weights and screened by the real blocklist, so rejections you see here are rejections your board would make.

What is not. Timings are the browser's, not the board's, which is why the speed control exists. The engine runs in double precision rather than the board's 32-bit floats, so a given seed will not reproduce the board's exact wording. The BOOT button goes dead while a fortune is being written, because on the board loop() is blocked inside makeFortune() and never polls the pin — that dead spell is real.
